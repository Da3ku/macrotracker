USDA FoodData Central

Free key at fdc.nal.usda.gov/api-key-signup. Open Food Facts covers barcodes and packaged goods; USDA covers whole foods (chicken breast, oats, rice) with far deeper micronutrient rows. DEMO_KEY works but throttles hard.

Data

Everything lives in IndexedDB on this device. Every record carries id + updatedAt + dirty, so a Supabase adapter can sync later without touching the UI. Export regularly — iOS evicts storage for web apps you haven't opened in a while, though installed home-screen apps are exempt.

Attribution
